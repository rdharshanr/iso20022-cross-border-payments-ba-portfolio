# 1. Business Problem

## Why do banks send Cross-Border Payments?

Banks facilitate cross-border payments to enable individuals, businesses, and financial institutions to securely transfer money across different countries. These payments support international trade, education, healthcare, tourism, investments, remittances, and global commerce.

Apart from providing essential financial services, banks also generate revenue through transaction fees, foreign exchange (FX) conversion, and premium international payment services while expanding their presence in global markets.

---

## What business problems exist today?

ABC International Bank currently operates on a legacy cross-border payment platform that has been in use for more than 15 years. Although the platform supports international payments, it faces several business and operational challenges.

Some of the major challenges include:

- Limited payment visibility for customers and operations teams.
- Delayed payment processing during peak transaction periods.
- High dependency on manual investigation for failed or delayed payments.
- Limited information available within payment messages.
- Increasing operational costs due to legacy infrastructure.
- Difficulty integrating with modern banking applications.
- Limited scalability to support future business growth.
- Challenges in meeting evolving regulatory and SWIFT standards.

These challenges impact customer experience, operational efficiency, and the bank's ability to compete in the modern payments ecosystem.

---

## Why is the bank modernizing?

ABC International Bank aims to modernize its cross-border payment platform to improve customer experience, increase operational efficiency, reduce manual intervention, and build a scalable payment ecosystem.

The modernization initiative will help the bank:

- Improve payment processing speed.
- Increase payment transparency.
- Reduce operational costs.
- Support future payment innovations.
- Improve regulatory compliance.
- Enable easier integration with internal and external systems.
- Build a future-ready payment platform based on industry standards.

---

## Why ISO 20022?

The existing payment messaging standard provides limited structured information, making payment processing, compliance checks, and investigations more complex.

ISO 20022 introduces a globally standardized XML-based messaging format that enables financial institutions to exchange richer and more structured payment information.

Key benefits include:

- Richer payment data.
- Improved Straight Through Processing (STP).
- Better interoperability between banks.
- Enhanced compliance and regulatory reporting.
- Improved payment tracking and transparency.
- Standardized messaging across financial institutions.

---

## Why REST APIs?

Modern banking platforms require multiple applications to communicate securely and efficiently. REST APIs provide a standardized way for systems such as Internet Banking, Core Banking, Payment Hub, AML, Sanctions, FX, and SWIFT Gateway to exchange information in real time.

The adoption of APIs enables:

- Faster system integration.
- Real-time payment processing.
- Improved payment tracking.
- Reduced manual intervention.
- Easier onboarding of new banking services.
- Better scalability and maintainability.
- Seamless communication between distributed banking applications.

# 2. Project Vision

ABC International Bank aims to transform its legacy cross-border payment platform into a modern, secure, scalable, and API-driven payment ecosystem based on ISO 20022 standards. The modernization program will enhance customer experience, improve operational efficiency, strengthen regulatory compliance, and enable seamless global payment processing for future business growth.

---

# 3. Success Criteria

The success of the modernization program will be measured based on the following outcomes:

- Reduce cross-border payment processing time.
- Improve payment transparency and end-to-end tracking.
- Support ISO 20022 standardized messaging.
- Increase Straight Through Processing (STP) rate.
- Reduce payment failures and manual interventions.
- Improve customer experience through faster payment status updates.
- Enable seamless integration using REST APIs.
- Strengthen AML and sanctions compliance.
- Improve payment investigation and return management.
- Build a scalable platform capable of supporting future payment services.
